## why

as someone who listens to music on telegram, it drives me nuts that music in
telegram channels is just one big list from top to bottom, no playlists, no
sorting by artist, none of the stuff any normal music platform has

so, out of that frustration, i made fygram

add a channel → it turns into a library with all the music ever posted
there, playable offline

- the only thing that goes through a middleman is your favorites, for
  syncing between devices
- you can sync music to your profile
- the music ducks in volume whenever telegram or another telegram fork
  makes a sound
- uses less ram than sc and spotify
